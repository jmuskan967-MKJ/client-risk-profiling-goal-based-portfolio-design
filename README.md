# Client Risk Profiling and Goal-Based Portfolio Design

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the application of wealth management concepts to client risk profiling and goal-based portfolio design. The project examines how an investor's financial profile, risk appetite, investment objectives, and goals can be considered while developing an appropriate portfolio strategy.

## Purpose

The purpose of this project was to understand and apply the process of:

- Assessing a client's risk profile
- Understanding risk appetite and investment preferences
- Identifying and prioritising financial goals
- Linking investment decisions with client objectives
- Developing an appropriate asset allocation strategy
- Applying diversification principles in portfolio construction
- Understanding the importance of portfolio review and monitoring

## Project Context

The project was undertaken as part of my experience as a **Wealth Management Intern at Prognosis Financial Private Limited**.

It provided practical exposure to the process of understanding client requirements and translating them into structured investment and portfolio-planning decisions.

## Approach / Methodology

The project followed a structured wealth management approach:

1. Understanding the client's financial profile and requirements
2. Assessing the client's risk appetite and risk capacity
3. Identifying the client's short-term and long-term financial goals
4. Considering investment horizon and return expectations
5. Developing an appropriate asset allocation approach
6. Applying diversification principles to portfolio construction
7. Aligning the portfolio with the client's identified goals
8. Considering the need for portfolio review and monitoring

The project helped develop an understanding of how investment decisions can be aligned with individual client characteristics rather than relying only on expected returns.

It provided practical exposure to the relationship between client profiling, financial goals, risk assessment, asset allocation, and portfolio construction in wealth management.
